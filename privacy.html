The small print

Privacy Policy

We keep this simple: we only ask for what we need to reply to you, send the dispatch if you want it, and get your decants to your door.

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Who we are

Robot Fragrances (“we”, “us”) sells perfume decants. If you have any question about this policy, email hello@robotfragrances.com.

What we collect

  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address, when you choose to subscribe.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, and the message you send us.
  • Orders: the details needed to fulfil an order — name, shipping address, email, and order contents. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • On your device: your shopping cart is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and mirrored into the page URL so it can be shared or restored. It never leaves your device unless you place an order. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or build a discovery set you asked for.
  • To send the newsletter — only if you opted in. You can unsubscribe anytime via the link in any email or by emailing us.
  • To process, pack, and ship your order, and to contact you about it.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data. We share it only with the services that make the shop work, for example:

  • a form-handling service that delivers contact and newsletter submissions to us;
  • an email/newsletter provider, if you subscribe;
  • Stripe, which processes payments. Card details are entered on Stripe’s own secure form and never reach our servers — we only ever see the last four digits and the outcome. See Stripe’s privacy policy;
  • shipping carriers, to deliver your order.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order records only as long as needed for the purpose above and any legal/accounting requirements, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@robotfragrances.com and we’ll action it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

Children

This shop is intended for adults. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the shop grows. We’ll change the “last updated” date above when we do.

Questions? Get in touch — a real person on the bench will reply.
