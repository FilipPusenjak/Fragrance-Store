Why decant

A full bottle is a big commitment.

Most fragrances are bought blind — a quick spray on paper, a hopeful purchase, and a beautiful bottle that lives out its days unloved on a shelf.

Decanting changes the order of things. Live with a scent through mornings and late nights, through warm days and cold ones, and then decide. We keep the experiment small so the discovery can be big.

  • Authentic juice, transferred — never reformulated or diluted.
  • Sizes from 2 ml testers to 10 ml everyday carries.
  • Full-bottle credit when a sample becomes a favourite.
