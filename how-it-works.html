The process

From sealed bottle
to your doorstep.

Decanting is simple, but doing it well takes care. Here's exactly how a fragrance travels from an authentic full bottle into the little atomiser that lands in your hands.

Source

We buy full bottles from authorised retailers and trusted distributors, then batch-check each one against the house's references before it earns a place on the bench.

Decant

Working in a clean, low-light space, we transfer the juice with sterilised tools — no heat, no dilution — and measure every fill to the millilitre so what you order is what you get.

Seal & ship

Each decant is capped in an airtight atomiser, labelled with name, house and batch, and packed snugly to arrive ready to wear.

Choose a size

How much is enough?

It depends on what you're after. A quick gut-check or a month-long trial — there's a size for each.

  • 2 ml tester — roughly 25 sprays. Perfect for a first impression.
  • 5 ml decant — about 65 sprays. Enough to live with a scent for weeks.
  • 10 ml carry — around 130 sprays. An everyday bottle for your bag.
  • 30 ml signature — roughly 390 sprays. A full-size pour for the scent you've committed to, on our designer and niche shelves.

Good to know

Quick questions

Is decanted perfume the real thing?

Yes. We transfer the exact juice from an authentic full bottle into a smaller atomiser. Nothing is added, diluted, or reformulated — it's the same fragrance, in a smaller vessel.

How long will a decant last?

Stored away from heat and direct light, a sealed decant keeps for years. Our atomisers are airtight to protect the juice in transit and on your shelf.

Do you offer full-bottle credit?

When a decant becomes a favourite, the amount you spent on samples of that scent can be credited toward a full bottle through us. Reach out and we'll set it up.
