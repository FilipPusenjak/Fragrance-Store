Our story

Precision, with
a sense of smell.

Robot Fragrances began with a simple frustration: loving the idea of a perfume, buying the bottle, and realising — too late — it wasn't the one.

The beginning

Built by people who kept buying the wrong bottle.

We're collectors first. Between us we'd accumulated a shelf of beautiful, expensive mistakes — fragrances that smelled incredible for an afternoon and wrong by the weekend.

Decanting was the obvious fix: smaller sizes, real juice, enough time to actually decide. So we set up a bench, dialled in the process, and started sharing it. The name is a wink at the part we obsess over — clean, repeatable, millimetre-accurate pours — paired with the very human job of finding a scent you love.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise.

Authenticity

Real bottles, verified batches, no exceptions. If we wouldn't wear it ourselves, it doesn't ship.

Precision

Measured fills, clean tools, consistent labels. The boring details are where trust is built.

Honesty

Fair prices, plain descriptions, and advice that sometimes means telling you not to buy.

“We'd rather sell you a $12 decant you adore than a $200 bottle you tolerate.”
— The Robot Fragrances bench
